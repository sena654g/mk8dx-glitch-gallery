Train Ride

Near the end of the course, you can drift off the stairs and jump to get wedged in the fence, dropping down onto the station platform. From there, you can enter the stairs and wait for the train, which runs on a cycle of about 2 minutes 3 seconds, then ride it. The train moves forward a short distance, stops, and after a few seconds, teleports one car at a time.This works in both 150cc and 200cc.
